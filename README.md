Open-world crime game on Puerto Luna island. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

Puerto Luna is a sun-soaked island city you explore on foot and by car. Rico Salas runs jobs for the fixer Mama Luz: seven story missions from stealing a sedan to holding the ferry terminal, plus a taxi side-job, a five-star wanted system with police chases, spike-strip roadblocks and hideouts, traffic, pedestrians, combat, a HUD with minimap, and a save that carries your cash and progress between sessions.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move and drive, E to enter and exit cars, mouse click to shoot, ESC to pause, M to mute.
